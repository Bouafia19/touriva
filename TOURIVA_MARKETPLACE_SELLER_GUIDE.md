## 1. Welcome to Touriva Marketplace

As a Touriva Partner, you gain a dedicated digital storefront, national order fulfillment infrastructure across all 58 wilayas, and transparent earnings statements.

## 2. Onboarding: Becoming a Verified Touriva Seller

### Step 1: Submitting Your Application
1. Visit the public storefront: `https://market.touriva.nextchallenge.tech/apply-seller`
2. Complete the merchant application form:
   - **Store Name:** Your brand name (e.g. *Sahara Expedition Outfitters*, *Artisans du M'Zab*).
   - **Contact Information:** Full name, mobile phone number, and business email.
   - **Craft / Product Category:** Select your primary domain (Outdoor Gear, Traditional Textiles, Ceramics & Pottery, Leathercraft, Sahara Terroir).
   - **Headquarters Wilaya:** Select your workshop/store wilaya.
   - **Artisan Story:** A brief introduction to your craft, provenance, and product range.
3. Submit the application.

### Step 2: Quality Review & Account Activation
Platform administration reviews your application within 24 to 48 hours. Upon approval, you will receive your partner sign-in credentials to access the **Seller Portal**.

---

## 3. Navigating the Seller Portal

1. Open: `https://seller.market.touriva.nextchallenge.tech`
2. Log in using your registered email and password.
3. Your **Seller Dashboard** provides an immediate overview of:
   - Incoming customer order items awaiting preparation.
   - Total sales volume and accumulated net earnings (DZD).
   - Catalog moderation status (Approved vs In Review).

---

## 4. Managing Your Product Catalog

### Adding a New Product
1. Click **My Products** (`/products`) in the sidebar navigation.
2. Click the **Add Product** button in the top right.
3. Enter your product details:
   - **Product Title:** Clear, descriptive name (e.g. *Tuareg Hand-Woven Wool Carpet (2x1.5m)*).
   - **Price (DZD):** Your retail price in Algerian Dinars.
   - **Promotional Price (DZD):** Optional discounted price for special sales.
   - **Available Stock Quantity:** Total physical units available in your workshop.
   - **Product Image URL:** High-quality photo showcasing the product clearly.
   - **Description:** Dimensions, materials used, regional origin (e.g. Djanet, Ghardaïa), and care instructions.
4. Click **Submit for Review**.

### Understanding Moderation Statuses
To guarantee high quality for travelers, all newly created and updated products pass through quality moderation:

| Status Badge | What it Means |
|---|---|
| **In Review** (Amber) | Your item is being reviewed by Touriva administration. It is not yet visible to public travelers. |
| **Approved & Live** (Green) | Your item has been approved and is actively visible for purchase on `market.touriva.nextchallenge.tech`. |
| **Rejected** (Red) | Your item was declined. Review the red feedback note explaining what needs to be adjusted (e.g. clearer photo, dimension details) before resubmitting. |

## 5. Order Fulfillment & Courier Shipping

When a customer purchases your item, it appears in your **Customer Orders** view (`/orders`).

### Fulfillment Workflow & 48-Hour SLA
Touriva requires merchant partners to prepare and hand over parcels to logistics couriers within **48 hours** of order notification.

```
+-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
| 1. PENDING        | ---> | 2. PREPARING      | ---> | 3. SHIPPED        | ---> | 4. DELIVERED      |
| Order received    |      | Item packaged &   |      | Handed to courier |      | Customer received |
| in your portal    |      | labeled           |      | with tracking #   |      | & confirmed       |
+-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
```

### Updating Order Status:
1. Navigate to **Customer Orders** (`/orders`).
2. Locate the order row and click **Update**.
3. Select the new status:
   - When packaging: Change status to **Preparing**.
   - When handed to courier: Change status to **Shipped**.
4. Enter the **Courier / Carrier** (e.g. *Yalidine Express*, *Procolis*, *EMS Algérie Poste*).
5. Enter the **Tracking Number / Bordereau** (e.g. *YAL-8839201*).
6. Click **Save Status**. The customer is automatically notified with their tracking code.

## 6. Financials, Commissions & Payouts

Touriva operates with transparent, per-item commission accounting.

### How Earnings are Calculated:
- When a customer purchases an item, your net payout equals:
  $$\text{Net Seller Payout} = \text{Item Sale Price} - (\text{Item Sale Price} \times \text{Commission Rate \%})$$
- *Example:* For a 10,000 DZD item with a standard 10% platform commission:
  - Gross Sale: 10,000 DZD
  - Touriva Platform Fee: 1,000 DZD
  - **Your Net Earnings:** 9,000 DZD

### Reviewing Your Earnings Statement:
1. Click **Earnings & Commissions** (`/finances`) in the sidebar.
2. View:
   - **Gross Volume:** Total customer purchases of your items.
   - **Commission Deducted:** Platform fees retained by Touriva.
   - **Net Seller Payout:** Your accumulated balance.
   - **Pending Settlement:** Earnings in process of bank/postal disbursement.
3. Disbursements are executed via **CCP** or **BaridiMob** to your designated account once delivery is confirmed.
